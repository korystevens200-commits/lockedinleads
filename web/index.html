How it works

From inquiry to booked job, without you touching it

Most leads go cold because nobody replies fast enough. LockedinLeads sits between your lead sources and your calendar and closes that gap automatically.

1

A lead comes in

Website form, Facebook or Google lead ad, a missed call you log by hand, or your CRM via webhook. Everything lands in one inbox.

2

It gets answered immediately

The assistant replies in seconds — day, night, weekend — using only the services, areas and pricing you configured.

3

It gets qualified

It asks your questions one at a time, checks the job is in your service area, and records every answer against the lead.

4

It gets booked

Qualified leads are offered real openings from your availability. You get a notification and the job appears on your calendar.

AI lead follow-up

The follow-ups you know you should be doing

Most people don't reply to the first message. LockedinLeads keeps going on a schedule you set — 15 minutes, 4 hours, tomorrow, three days — and stops the moment the lead replies, books, opts out, or you close them.

  • Follow-up timing you control, per account
  • Held until business hours if you prefer
  • Full conversation context carried through every message
  • Hard stop on opt-out — nobody gets spammed

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Will the AI say something wrong to my customers?
It can only state what you configure — your services, your service areas, your pricing notes and real openings from your calendar. If a customer asks something outside that, it says it will confirm with the team and flags the conversation for you rather than guessing.
Can I take over a conversation myself?
Yes. Every conversation has an AI Active / Human Takeover switch. Flip it, or just send a message yourself, and the assistant stops immediately. Hand it back whenever you want and the follow-up schedule resumes.
What lead sources does it work with?
Your website form, Facebook and Instagram lead ads, Google Ads lead forms, Google Business, Zapier, and most CRMs via webhook. Calls you take yourself can be entered by hand in a few seconds — the assistant picks it up from there.
How long does setup take?
The guided setup is seven steps: your business details, services, service areas, qualification questions, booking rules, lead sources, and a test conversation. Most of the work is deciding what you want asked — we do it with you on the setup call.
What happens if a lead says stop?
They're opted out immediately and permanently. No further automated or manual messages can be sent to them from the system, and the lead is closed out.
Do I need to be technical?
No. Everything the assistant does — how it talks, what it asks, when it follows up, what it can book — is edited in plain forms in the dashboard. There's nothing to install and no code to write.
Is there a contract?
Month to month after the one-time setup fee. Cancel any time; you keep every lead and conversation record in your account until you ask us to delete it.
